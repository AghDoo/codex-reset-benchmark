Methodology 1.1

Target

The benchmark scores whether a new qualifying broad/global public Codex usage-limit reset event occurs within 5h, 24h, or 48h after a formal checkpoint. Personal rolling resets, banked credits, reset-button hints, vague promises, and individual quota events are excluded.

Formal checkpoints

00:00, 06:00, 12:00, and 18:00 UTC. A 5h forecast must be no more than one hour old; 24h/48h forecasts may be no more than six hours old. High-frequency publishers therefore do not receive extra weight.

Common-case ranking

Methodology 1.1 ranks eligible sources within a horizon only on the intersection of resolved checkpoints where every source in that ranking cohort has a fresh forecast. Missing forecasts are never imputed. This prevents a source from benefiting by missing difficult checkpoints.

Score samples are common cases used for official metrics. Coverage counts all fresh resolved cases a source actually supplied; availability remains source-specific.

Primary metric

Brier = (p - o)². Lower is better. Calibration, log loss, binary hit rate, availability, score samples, and coverage are shown as diagnostics. At least 10 common score cases are required before an official rank is assigned.

Ground truth

Only reviewed status=confirmed events in data/events/resets.json are scored. A case is not resolved until the complete forecast window falls at or before the dataset's reviewed_at boundary. Banked reset grants and signal-only announcements are retained as excluded audit evidence but never count as positive outcomes.

Auditability

Forecast NDJSON is append-only. Full third-party pages are not republished; the archive stores normalized probability values, timestamps, source URLs, collector metadata, and a SHA-256 of the retrieved representation.
