Methodology

Target

V1 scores whether a new qualifying broad/global public Codex usage-limit reset event occurs within 5h, 24h, or 48h after a formal checkpoint. Personal rolling resets, banked credits, vague promises, and individual quota events are excluded.

Common checkpoints

00:00, 06:00, 12:00, and 18:00 UTC. A 5h forecast must be no more than one hour old; 24h/48h forecasts may be no more than six hours old. High-frequency publishers therefore do not receive extra weight.

Primary metric

Brier = (p - o)². Lower is better. Calibration, log loss, binary hit rate, availability, and sample count are shown as diagnostics. A horizon needs 10 resolved samples before its rank becomes official.

Ground truth

Only reviewed status=confirmed events in data/events/resets.json are scored. When an independently verifiable effective time is unavailable, an explicit completed reset announcement time is used as the observable event time.

Auditability

Forecast NDJSON is append-only. Full third-party pages are not republished; the archive stores normalized probability values, timestamps, source URLs, collector metadata, and a SHA-256 of the retrieved representation.
