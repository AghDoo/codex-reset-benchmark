How scoring works

Forecasts may be archived hourly, but are scored only at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, and 18:00 UTC. A 5h forecast must be no more than one hour old; 24h and 48h forecasts may be no more than six hours old. Horizons are scored separately with mean Brier Score as the primary metric.
